Champions League Matchday 5: Arsenal–Dortmund and Man City–Napoli Headline Tuesday's Fixtures

Matchday 5 brings another important round of the 2026–27 Champions League league phase, featuring Arsenal vs Borussia Dortmund, Manchester City vs Napoli and Real Madrid vs PSV Eindhoven on 24 November.

A busy night in the Champions League

The 2026–27 UEFA Champions League league phase continues on Tuesday, 24 November, with Matchday 5 featuring several high-profile fixtures. Arsenal host Borussia Dortmund, Manchester City welcome Napoli and Real Madrid face PSV Eindhoven.

With the league phase still in progress, each match offers teams another chance to improve their position before the final rounds.

Three fixtures drawing attention

Arsenal vs Borussia Dortmund is one of the standout meetings, bringing together two clubs with strong European ambitions. The tactical battle in midfield and the ability to manage transitions could be important.

Manchester City vs Napoli is another major fixture. City's control of possession will be tested by Napoli's ability to press and break quickly.

Real Madrid vs PSV Eindhoven completes the trio of headline games. Madrid's experience in European competition meets a PSV side looking to make an impression on a major stage.

Other matches on the schedule

Tuesday's programme also includes Bodø/Glimt vs LASK, Galatasaray vs Aston Villa, Como vs AEK Athens, Feyenoord vs Porto, Leipzig vs Lens and Slovan Bratislava vs Real Betis.

Why Matchday 5 matters

The league phase runs through 27 January 2027, so there is still time for the table to change. However, points earned now can reduce pressure in the closing rounds and give teams a clearer route toward qualification.

The takeaway

Tuesday's schedule offers a mix of heavyweight European meetings and important tests for clubs chasing progress. Arsenal–Dortmund, City–Napoli and Real Madrid–PSV are the fixtures most likely to attract global attention.
