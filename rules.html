Exchange Betting Rules

Learn how market odds, fancy bets, sessions, and match conditions are settled across our partner exchanges.

General Match Settlement Rules

All cricket match bets are settled strictly according to the official scores and decisions announced by the governing authority (e.g., ICC, BCCI, Cricket Australia) at the conclusion of the match:

  • Super Over / Boundary Count: In limited overs cricket (T20 and ODI), if a match ends in a tie and a Super Over is played, match winner bets are settled according to the Super Over result unless specified otherwise in the market rules.
  • Rain / Weather Interruption (DLS Method): In matches shortened by rain where an official result is declared via the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern (DLS) method, match winner bets stand as settled.
  • Abandoned Matches: If a match is abandoned with no balls bowled or before minimum overs are completed without an official result, all match odds bets are voided and stakes refunded.

Session & Fancy Betting Rules

Session bets (such as 6 over, 10 over, 15 over, 20 over runs) provide some of the fastest-paced excitement on RAMJI247:

  • Completed Overs Requirement: For a session to be valid, all overs in that session bracket must be bowled, or the batting side must be bowled out, or the target reached.
  • Rain Interruption in Sessions: If rain interrupts the match before the session overs are completed and match overs are reduced, that specific incomplete session is declared void.
  • Runs Counted: All legitimate runs, extras (wides, no balls, byes, leg-byes, penalty runs) count towards session totals.

Toss Prediction Rules

Toss markets are settled the instant the official on-field coin toss is completed. If the match is delayed or postponed after the toss has occurred, all toss bets remain valid as the coin toss has concluded.
